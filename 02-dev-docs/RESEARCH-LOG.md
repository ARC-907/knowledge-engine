# Research Log

> Session-level activity log for content/research changes inside `corpus/`. Append as you populate the corpus and as research activities occur.

## Starting state

- The repo ships a demo corpus under `corpus/samples/`: `demo-library/`
  (three-lens **CONCEPT → PATTERN → APPLY**) and `demo-skill/`. Both are
  enabled in `corpus/registry.json` so `knowledge-engine reindex` has real
  content to index on a fresh clone.
- The demo content is *demonstration* material — it shows the three-lens
  organizational methodology. Replace it with your own content; see
  [`BRING-YOUR-OWN-CORPUS.md`](../01-user-docs/BRING-YOUR-OWN-CORPUS.md).
- This log resets per workspace; use it the same way you'd use a per-library
  `RESEARCH-LOG.md` — append an entry as you add, revise, or research content.
